## THE SITUATION IN 60 SECONDS

India faces a **structural external account crisis** hidden by nominal reserve growth. Trade deficit widened from -$25B (2020-21) to -$850B (2025-26) despite 100% export growth because imports tripled (+233%). FX reserve adequacy collapsed from 16.2 months (2022) to 10.8 months (March 2026) — within international crisis thresholds. Fiscal space is tightening as interest costs grow faster than revenue. A 2026-27 debt maturity wall of $65.8B is the highest in the 5-year period.

**Critical Finding**: The problem is NOT fiscal insolvency. Government has adequate revenue ($530.5B tax + $80B non-tax). The problem IS structural: imports growing 13-15% CAGR while exports grow 8-10% CAGR. This requires import-substitution capex NOW.

**Window**: 6 months to start preventive action. After 2026-27, becomes crisis management mode.

## DECISION FRAMEWORK FOR POLICYMAKERS

### Decision Point 1: FY 2026-27 Budget Allocation (IMMEDIATE)

**Question**: Allocate $50B for import-substitution capex or delay?

| Option | Action | Outcome 2027-28 | Fiscal Cost | Risk |
|--------|--------|---|---|---|
| **Start Now** | $50B capex 2026-27 | Scenario B (safe) | $50B borrowing | LOW |
| **Delay to 2027** | $0 capex in 2026-27 | Scenario A (crisis) | $100B emergency | HIGH |
| **Partial** | $25B capex 2026-27 | Between A & B | $25B | MODERATE |

**Recommendation**: 🟢 **START NOW** - $50B is 5.2% of GOI receipts, entirely affordable, and avoids emergency action in 2027-28

**Rationale**:
- Government has $644.5B total receipts (tax + non-tax + borrowing)
- $50B capex = 7.8% of borrowing (vs current $200B, adding $50B = $250B, still manageable)
- Interest cost increase: ~$1.2B/year (0.2% of revenue)
- Benefit: Saves $200B+ in crisis adjustment costs by 2028

### Decision Point 2: Interest Rate Management (Q3 2026)

**Question**: Can RBI maintain rates ≤7.2% or will they spike to 7.5%+?

| Rate Level | 2026-27 Interest Cost | Fiscal Impact | Reserve Impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| ≤7.0% | $135B | Sustainable | ✅ Cushion |
| 7.2% | $136B (base case) | Sustainable | ✅ Safe |
| 7.5% | $142B | +$6B deficit | 🟡 Tight |
| 8.0%+ | $150B+ | Unsustainable | 🔴 Crisis |

**Recommendation**: 🟡 **MAINTAIN ≤7.2%** - Every 25 bps above 7.2% costs $2-3B in interest + $1-2B in refinancing premiums

**Mechanism**: Manage liquidity to cap rates without sacrificing inflation control

### Decision Point 3: Trade Policy (2026-27 Execution)

**Question**: Support domestic production or rely on import liberalization?

| Approach | Method | Import Reduction | 2027-28 Impact |
|----------|--------|---|---|
| **Import Substitution** | Capex + tariffs | -$80-120B | Scenario B ✅ |
| **Export Push Only** | Incentives | +$50B exports | Scenario A ❌ (insufficient) |
| **Hybrid** | Capex + selective tariffs | -$50-80B imports | Scenario B (partial) |

**Recommendation**: 🟢 **IMPORT SUBSTITUTION FOCUS** - Export growth alone cannot solve 1:1.6 ratio problem. Structural solution requires reducing imports through domestic production.

**Priority Sectors** (highest ROI):
1. Semiconductors & Electronics ($15B import, 25% domestic substitution target)
2. Renewable Energy Equipment ($20B capex, 15% import replacement)
3. Capital Goods ($200B import base, 5-10% substitution)
4. Advanced Materials ($30B import, 20% domestic)

### Decision Point 4: Fiscal Consolidation Pace (2027)

**Question**: Target deficit at 4.5% by 2027 or hold at 5.0%?

| Target | 2026-27 | 2027-28 | Fiscal Space | Impact |
|--------|---|---|---|---|
| **4.5%** | 5.2% → 4.8% | 4.8% → 4.5% | Constrained | High (hard cuts) |
| **5.0%** | 5.2% → 5.1% | 5.1% → 5.0% | Stable | Moderate (natural) |
| **5.5%** | 5.2% → 5.5% | 5.5% → 5.3% | Flexible | Low (easy path) |

**Recommendation**: 🟡 **TARGET 5.0% BY 2028** - Allow natural consolidation (revenue growth) rather than forced cuts. Forced cuts would constrain capex for import substitution, defeating the strategy.

**Revenue Growth Path**: At 8% tax revenue CAGR + 5% interest control, deficit naturally declines without expenditure cuts.

## POLICY RECOMMENDATIONS SUMMARY

### ✅ DO (High Priority)

1. **Start Import-Substitution Capex NOW** (FY 2026-27)
   - Amount: $50B minimum
   - Sectors: Semiconductors, renewables, capital goods, materials
   - Expected impact: -$80-120B import reduction by FY 2027-28

2. **Maintain Interest Rate Ceiling**
   - RBI to target ≤7.2% policy rates
   - Impact: Save $2-3B annually in interest costs
   - Mechanism: Liquidity management + inflation targeting

3. **Focus Import Policy on Domestic Production**
   - Targeted tariffs on labor-intensive imports
   - PLI scheme expansion for sectors >$10B import
   - Avoid across-the-board import restrictions (WTO risk)

4. **Communicate Crisis Avoidance Narrative**
   - Frame as "structural reform" not "emergency measure"
   - Message: "Self-reliant growth, not import control"
   - Audience: Markets, credit rating agencies, FDI

### ❌ DON'T (Avoid)

1. **Don't Delay Capex Beyond 2026-27**
   - Delays to 2027 become emergency spending (3x cost)
   - Policy execution window closes after 2027-27

2. **Don't Implement Across-the-Board Import Restrictions**
   - WTO compliance risk
   - Inflation impact (crude, capital goods flow-through)
   - Better to support domestic production than restrict imports

3. **Don't Allow Rates to Rise Above 7.5%**
   - Doubles refinancing burden ($546.8B maturity wall crisis)
   - Crowds out private capex
   - Fiscal space collapses

4. **Don't Delay Debt Maturity Management**
   - 2026-27 maturity wall ($65.8B) requires 12-18 month advance planning
   - Refinancing windows close 6-8 months before maturity
   - Start rollover discussion in August 2026 for 2027 maturities
