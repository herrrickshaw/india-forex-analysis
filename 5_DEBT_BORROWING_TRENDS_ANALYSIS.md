## 🚨 2026-27 Maturity Wall

**Bonds due for repayment**: ₹546.8B = $65.8B (HIGHEST IN 5-YEAR PERIOD)

**What this means:**
- Pandemic stimulus bonds (2020-2023) now maturing
- Must be rolled over or paid from reserves
- If interest rates elevated: Refinancing costs spike
- Government may need higher yields to refinance

## Debt Sustainability Rating

### 🟢 Positive Factors
✓ Borrowing at manageable 31% of receipts

✓ Market capacity strong (can raise more if needed)

### 🟡 Risk Factors
