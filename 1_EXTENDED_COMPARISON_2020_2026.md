## Projection Methodology

**Approach**: CAGR-based extrapolation with conservative caps on extreme growth rates

### Projection Rules
1. **Sustainable commodities** (CAGR <100%): Use historical CAGR
2. **High-growth** (CAGR 100-300%): Cap at 50% forward
3. **Extreme growth** (CAGR >300%): Cap at 30% forward
4. **Emerging** (zero baseline): Project from 2023 base +20% annually

## Conclusion

**Verified Growth 2020-2023**: India's exports grew 44.1% ($623B → $898B)

**Conservative 2026 Projection**: $1.08-1.30T total exports
- Additional $180-400B growth from 2023 baseline
- Driven primarily by sustainable sectors
- Recovery-phase commodities may moderate

**Strategic Recommendation**:
- **Strong sectors** (vehicles, pharma, chemicals): 15-30% CAGR sustainable through 2026
- **Emerging sectors**: Monitor for scaling potential
- **Mature sectors**: Plan for flat-to-declining growth

**Investment Thesis**: India's export sector continues strong growth trajectory with diversification into high-value sectors, positioned for $1.1-1.3T total value by 2026.
